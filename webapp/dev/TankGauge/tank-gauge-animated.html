Why this matters: a 4-point sensor doesn't measure continuously — it tells you which switch the water has reached. A "75%" reading means the tank is at least 75% full, but it could be anywhere up to completely full. Once you calibrate each threshold to actual liters, swap the % labels below for the measured litre values and the same interval logic still holds. For now, a 0% (no-signal) reading is treated as "level is somewhere between 0–25%" — worth revisiting once you have real sensor data, since a dead reading could in principle also mean the sensor itself lost power or a wire came loose.
